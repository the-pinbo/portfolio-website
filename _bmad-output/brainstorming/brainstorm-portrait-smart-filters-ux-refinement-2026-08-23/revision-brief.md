# Portrait and Smart Filters UX Revision Brief

## Design principle

Use **separation, then registration** as the shared interaction grammar. Each visual should communicate its underlying idea rather than decorate it: one portrait source is divided across controlled masks, and one query resolves into structured filters. Every major section must first read clearly and feel premium as a static frame; motion may only enhance an already-resolved composition.

## Strengths to preserve

### Slide 2

- Coherent paper, navy, and coral palette.
- Memorable portrait and recognizable three-panel silhouette.
- Prominent name, confident negative space, and absence of competing buttons.

### Slide 3

- Paper, navy, and coral continuity.
- Readable two-column composition, improved copy hierarchy, and spaciousness.
- The specific `DATE / Last week` and `TRACKER / Cancellation` rows.

Also preserve the approved opening thesis frame, broader site architecture, palette, and navigation concept during this revision.

## Slide 2 required changes

- Rebuild the portrait from one shared, stage-sized image behind three geometrically aligned masks.
- Show the full face only in the central pillar.
- Use the side pillars for distinct coordinates containing background brushwork, hair edges, shoulders or clothing, the coral shape, and small image extensions; do not duplicate facial fragments or imply a second person.
- Reduce the resolved identity to the name and `SDE II at RingCentral`.
- Remove the repeated explanatory sentence and full-uppercase metadata-banner treatment.
- Remove the active Work state before the Work section begins.
- Keep the pillars aligned and stable.

## Slide 3 required changes

- Replace the decorative Smart Filters architecture with a direct query-to-interface transformation.
- Extract `last week` and `cancellation` from the sentence and resolve them into `DATE / Last week` and `TRACKER / Cancellation` using only minimal registration lines or ink trails.
- Use the headline `Make complex filters easy to use.`
- Use `People describe the calls they need. The interface handles the filters.` as smaller, medium-weight supporting copy.
- Shorten the technical metadata to deterministic routing, model-assisted interpretation, evaluation, and observability.
- Remove the giant background circle, navy central disc, concentric rings, coral dotted orbit, decorative connector lines, random diagonals, revision pill, and `INTERPRET · ROUTE · MEASURE`.

## Interaction rules

- Motion follows the approved sequence: separation, then registration.
- Portrait geometry remains stable; only ink layers may separate subtly, halftone detail may sharpen, and contrast may respond before returning to register.
- Query phrases may detach from the sentence and settle into their corresponding filter rows.
- Motion must clarify the same meaning already visible in the static composition.
- No section may depend on hover or animation to feel sophisticated or understandable.

## Static acceptance criteria

- Slide 2 reads as one portrait source divided intentionally across three aligned masks.
- The face is central and is not duplicated in either side pillar.
- Slide 2 identity contains only the name and `SDE II at RingCentral`.
- No premature active navigation state appears.
- Slide 3 makes the sentence-to-filter transformation understandable without animation.
- The approved headline, quieter supporting line, specific resolved rows, and simplified technical metadata are present.
- No decorative pseudo-architecture remains.
- Both revised frames read clearly and feel premium as still images before motion is designed or implemented.
