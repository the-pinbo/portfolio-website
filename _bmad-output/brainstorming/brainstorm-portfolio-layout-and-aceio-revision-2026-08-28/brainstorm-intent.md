# Portfolio Layout and ACE Revision Intent

## Objective

Revise the portfolio as one coherent editorial system with chapter-specific evidence. Complete a fresh UX audit before changing UX contracts or mocks. Preserve Slide 2 exactly.

## Global rules

- Use a single 1280px editorial frame with 12 columns and 24px gutters.
- Give copy and visuals a consistent top anchor.
- Keep body copy left-aligned with a deliberate rag and a stable 52–56ch measure. Do not justify paragraphs.
- Use compact headlines, intentional line wrapping, stronger spacing, and smaller right-aligned visuals.
- Visible portfolio copy must not contain em dashes.
- Treat chapter-specific composition as a restrained exception within the shared grid, not as a separate slide system.

## ACE naming and framing

- `ACE` is the only public product name.
- Remove `RingSense` completely.
- Never use `ACE.io` publicly; it is only the hackathon name.
- ACE is the product. Do not describe ACE itself as an MCP app.
- Describe the MCP app as an offering from ACE.
- Use the exact chapter headline: **Bring the product into the AI workspace.**
- Use the supporting line: **ACE offers an MCP app that brings governed data, permissions, and product workflows into the AI workspace.**
- Replace the current four-node relay with a compact two-layer product model: governed capability underneath and the MCP app experience above.
- Reduce the visual so it fits its frame and correct the current right-heavy alignment.
