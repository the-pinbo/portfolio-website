- (decision) Experience target: quietly exceptional. The portfolio should create an immediate sense of professional craft and real engineering investment, with Apple- and Google-caliber minimalism. Interactivity is purposeful and technically credible, never cluttered or decorative; Antigravity is a primary structural and motion reference, without copying its brand or content.
- (decision) Hero choreography: begin with the thesis alone. Only after the statement resolves does it move aside, creating space for the portrait to enter. The portrait is an earned reveal, not a simultaneous split-screen asset.
- (decision) Resolved-hero content: portrait plus name, current title ‘SDE II’, and one understated line such as ‘Building intelligent systems from ambiguity to production.’ Preserve large negative space and strong typography. No competing hero buttons.
- (decision) Visual identity: modern Riso-tech editorial, not retro pixel or voxel. Derive the palette from the portrait: deep ink navy, muted coral-red, soft paper, and cool gray-blue for structure. Coral is the single interactive accent. Translate ‘pixel’ into dither, halftone, and low-resolution image transitions rather than blocks. Concentrate paper fibre, grain, and ink-imperfection texture in the hero, image masks, and occasional project moments; keep the wider layout clean. Typography direction: Instrument Sans for display/body/UI and IBM Plex Mono for technical details.
- (decision) Theme: light-only in v1. Use a soft, subtly textured paper canvas throughout; no light/dark toggle. The portrait’s printed paper quality should integrate naturally with the page rather than being isolated inside a contrasting treatment.
- (decision) Information architecture: v1 is a single, continuous, scroll-led narrative. Navigation offers restrained section shortcuts but does not turn the portfolio into a set of separate project pages.
- (decision) Interactive visual systems are separate. (1) Portrait: retain the three-pillar clipping mask, with a dominant central portrait and two narrower supporting crops. Each pillar may respond independently on hover. Refine proportions and spacing later, but preserve the core three-panel identity. (2) WebGL: include a distinct, minimal Antigravity-inspired atmospheric system elsewhere in the hero/scroll experience; WebGL is not limited to or dependent on the portrait hover.
- (decision) Typography refinement: remove the handwritten caption from the portrait and remove handwritten fonts from the website entirely. Use modern sans typography for display/body/UI and IBM Plex Mono for technical metadata; human imperfection comes from Riso texture, halftone, and ink registration rather than script lettering.
- (decision) Project storytelling hierarchy: replace the all-project horizontal rail as the primary treatment. Smart Filters receives a full scroll-led flagship chapter; ACE io MCP and VisionGuard follow as shorter immersive chapters. A compact horizontal rail may appear afterward for secondary projects and breadth.
- (decision) Case-study asset strategy: create original, theme-consistent editorial illustrations for confidential projects and as art-directed covers for public work. Generated visuals use the Riso-tech palette, texture, and print language; they are illustrative, never fabricated UI, fake dashboards, or invented evidence. Real repositories, papers, certificates, screenshots, documents, and links remain visually distinct and carry proof. VisionGuard may combine an original Riso editorial cover with real public artifacts beneath it.
- (decision) Project-image principle: each case-study visual must read as a compelling conceptual editorial composition from a distance and reveal technically meaningful structure on closer inspection. Avoid decoration without informational value.
- (decision) Navigation behavior: hide navigation during the opening thesis. When the thesis resolves and the portrait enters, reveal a compact fixed navigation with Work, Research, Track Record, and Contact. Navigation remains a quiet shortcut layer over the single-page story.
- (decision) Motion structure: use exactly two major pinned scroll sequences. First, the opening thesis transforms into portrait and identity. Second, Smart Filters unfolds from business friction into a dependable production system. All other sections use natural document scrolling with restrained, motivated reveals.
- (decision) Responsive motion: mobile preserves the same narrative order and three-pillar portrait composition but removes scroll pinning. WebGL becomes lightweight or static; hover-only portrait behavior becomes a brief automatic ink-registration reveal. Touch and reduced-motion experiences remain complete without motion.
- (override) Source-link presentation: reject visible umbrella labels such as ‘Proof’, ‘Evidence’, ‘Public summary’, and ‘Internal implementation confidential’. These feel defensive and clichéd. Sourceability should be integrated quietly into each content object; do not foreground the absence of public RingCentral artifacts.
- (decision) Linked-artifact pattern: integrate source links directly beside the relevant work with a restrained icon and literal noun such as Code, Write-up, Paper, Patent, or Certificate. No shared heading, container, badge, or credibility language. Icon-label links remain readable on desktop and accessible by name on touch and assistive technology.
- (decision) Post-project structure: Research, Open Source, and Patent are separate compact editorial chapters, not a combined achievement grid. Each chapter uses one strong visual idea, minimal copy, and directly integrated artifact links.
- (decision) Track Record treatment: use a large typographic index rather than a chronological résumé timeline. Order by narrative importance: RingCentral, IISc, GSoC, Linux Foundation, Goldman Sachs, Determinant Studios, NITK. Each entry shows institution, role/relationship, year, and one concise contribution; hover/focus may reveal a related real artifact or image.
- (decision) Capabilities presentation: omit a conventional Skills section and skill-cloud UI. Reveal relevant technologies as quiet monospace metadata inside project chapters. Summarize the core once as AI/ML Systems, Backend & Platform Engineering, Evaluation & Observability, and Production Delivery. Product judgment and interface experience appear only as supporting strengths demonstrated within stories.
- (decision) Closing behavior: end with the direct line ‘If you’re building something difficult, I’d like to hear about it.’ Use one primary Email me action, followed by quiet LinkedIn and GitHub links. No contact form or competing calls to action.
- (override) Hard anti-patterns: remove the Engineering Loop and any generic methodology/process section. No templated Projects-Skills-Experience-Contact composition; every section must advance one authored narrative. No skill-logo walls, technology marquees, percentage bars, proficiency ratings, custom cursor, generic process pillars, equal project-card grids, self-awarded credibility badges, or cliché labels such as Featured, Award Winning, Proof, Verified, and Production. Avoid generic filler copy. Technologies appear only where they explain a real project decision.
- (decision) Typography and copy hierarchy: replace the thesis with ‘I turn meaningful business problems into reliable software and AI systems.’ followed by the quieter cadence ‘Evaluated. Integrated. Shipped.’ Never use display scale as a substitute for hierarchy. No overlapping text, handwritten typography, oversized multi-line section headings, or tiny identity text competing with enormous slogans. Remove visible labels and phrases including Engineering Loop, What I Own, Flagship Systems, production-ready, and defensive confidentiality copy. Project ownership must be communicated naturally within the narrative.
- (decision) Typography hierarchy baseline: opening thesis 52-72px desktop and 2-3 lines maximum; resolution cadence 18-22px; resolved name 48-64px and visually dominant; role/identity 16-20px; section headings 36-48px and normally one line; project names 40-56px; body 17-19px at 55-65 characters per line; technical metadata 12-14px IBM Plex Mono used sparingly. No overlap. Display scale must be planned around content width and imagery.
